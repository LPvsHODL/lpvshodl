How much do you need for a pool to be worth it?

Every position costs gas twice — once to open, once to close — and twice more each time you move your range. Below a certain deposit those costs eat the fees entirely. This works out where that line sits, using what each pool actually paid over the last year rather than an advertised rate.

Gas is entered per chain because that is where the difference actually lives: the same transaction can cost pennies on one chain and tens of dollars on another. Charging every pool the same figure would make the cheap chains look far worse than they are, which is the sort of thing that quietly ruins a ranking. Nothing is filled in for you — check what a swap costs right now on each chain and put that number in. Opening and closing is 2 transactions; each re-centre is 2 more.
